# The Problem with Scattered Learning Resources

If you've ever tried to learn a new skill online, you know the feeling. You bookmark articles, save tutorials, and collect videos—only to lose track of them within days. Your learning resources end up scattered across bookmarks, browser tabs, and notes apps. There's no structure, no sequence, and no easy way to share what you've found with others.

This is the problem Linkversity solves.

## The Chaos of Unorganized Links

Most learners face these common challenges:

- **Bookmark overload**: Hundreds of saved links with no clear organization
- **No sequence**: Links are saved randomly, not in a logical learning order
- **Hard to share**: Can't easily send someone a structured learning path
- **Content gets lost**: Useful resources disappear into the bookmark graveyard
- **No progress tracking**: Can't see how far you've come or what's next

## How Linkversity Helps

Linkversity lets you turn scattered links into structured **Learning Paths**. Here's how:

### 1. Create Ordered Sections

Organize your resources into sections with a logical sequence. Start with beginner content, move to intermediate, and finish with advanced materials. Each section builds on the previous one.

### 2. Add Any Link Type

You can add:
- YouTube videos and tutorials
- Blog posts and articles
- Documentation and guides
- GitHub repositories
- Online courses
- Podcasts and more

### 3. Share with Anyone

Your learning paths are shareable via a simple URL. Perfect for:
- Onboarding new team members
- Helping friends learn new skills
- Creating curriculum for students
- Documenting your learning journey

### 4. Track Progress

Mark sections as complete and see your progress. Know exactly where you are and what's next.
